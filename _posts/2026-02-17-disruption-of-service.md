Unfortunately, api.met.no (along with most other services from MET Norway) was
unavailable yesterday 16 Feb between 15 and 16 UTC, due to a loss of Internet
connection at our Oslo office. Apparently this was caused by some scheduled
router maintenance at our ISP which failed, without triggering the failover
to our other line. We are very sorry but this was out of our control.
